# Context

## Problem / goal

Landing goal: sale  
Visitors want café-quality coffee at home without settling for supermarket beans or paying café prices every day. The page should move them to buy Athena coffee (beans or ground) in one clear path.

## Landing type

selling

## Theme

`ecommerce_product`  
R cells from the theme→elements matrix apply: `hero_offer`, `product_offer`, `review_summary`. Explicit waive required if any R is skipped later.

## Audience familiarity

cold  
Most visitors do not know Athena; the page needs a short “why us / what this coffee is” without a deep roast-education essay.

## Brand / product

**Athena** — specialty coffee roasting and retail (whole bean / ground) for people who want a café cup at home: fresh roast, readable flavor profile, no snobby ritual.

## Audience and pain

Urban coffee drinkers ~25–40 who are tired of mediocre supermarket coffee and expensive café runs; they want reliable taste at home without a complicated specialty hobby.

## Primary CTA

Label: **Купить**  
Destination: in-page product offer block (SKU card / shop section on the same page). Not a lead form as the primary action. Checkout may be an external link or stub at brief time.

## Price

Placement: product block (after benefits / offer framing), not in the first-viewport hero.  
Specificity: exact price per pack.

## Constraints

Tone: Russian **ты**, calm, no snobbery, no fake barista jargon.  
Bans: fake testimonials; medical claims.  
No legal extras or hard deadlines unless added later.

## Motion

`load-atmosphere`

## Deploy and stack

Default: GitHub Pages + prerendered static HTML.  
No server-side backend required for the landing; purchase is external link or checkout stub.

## Analytics

none

## Canonical terms

- **Brand:** Athena  
- **Offer:** specialty coffee (beans / ground), fresh roast, clear flavor, café-at-home without snobbery  
- **Primary CTA:** Купить → in-page product offer  
- **Theme:** ecommerce_product  
- **Motion:** load-atmosphere
